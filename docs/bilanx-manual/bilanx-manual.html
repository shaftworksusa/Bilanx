02

Mounting instructions

Warning

We strongly recommend having this product installed by a qualified shop.

Warning

If you work with a lifted vehicle, make sure it is safely supported so it cannot tip over.

Note

See the vehicle service manual for vehicle-specific procedures and torque values.

  1. Raise the vehicle and put it on jack stands at the factory lift points.
  2. Remove the wheels.
  3. Remove the upper and lower attachments and remove the original shock absorbers.
  4. Install the Bilanx coilovers on the vehicle.
  5. Fasten the upper and lower attachments. Tighten to the factory torque values.
  6. Secure brake hoses and ABS wires so they cannot touch the coilover.
  7. Reinstall all removed parts the same way they were before installation.
  8. Set ride height and check bump travel and droop before driving (see Adjustments).
  9. Have the vehicle aligned.
Note

Make sure all bolts are tightened to the correct torque, and that nothing fouls or restricts the coilover at full compression, full extension and full steering lock.

03

Adjustments

Warning

Before you drive, make sure the setup matches the recommended setup data. Contact Shaftworks USA if you have any questions about setting up.

Your recommended damping and spring rates are on the setup sheet in the box.

Check your dampers for a "Do not adjust" sticker.

Do not adjust Sticker present

Damper length has been set for your car. Do not move the lower mount. Set ride height with the spring seat only.

No sticker

Damper length is not pre-set. You must check bump travel and droop for your car before driving.

Adjust in this order: ride height and travel, then damping, then tuning. Re-check alignment after any height change.

Ride height and travel

Ride height is set with the spring seat. Damper length is set at the lower mount and controls how travel is split between bump (compression) and droop (extension). A loose or preloaded spring at your chosen height is normal and does not change how the car handles.

  1. Loosen the lock ring with the supplied spanner.
  2. Wind the spring seat up to raise the car, or down to lower it. Match left and right.
  3. Tighten the lock ring against the seat.
  4. Settle the car (roll it back and forth), then measure hub center to fender lip at each corner.

Check droop: keep at least 50 mm below ride height

  1. Measure tire bottom to fender at ride height.
  2. Lift both wheels on that axle off the ground and measure again.
  3. The difference is your droop.

Check bump travel: keep at least 35 mm at ride height

  1. At ride height, slide the bump stop down the shaft until it touches the damper body.
  2. Fit a zip tie on the shaft against the seal.
  3. Drive the car normally (or a few laps), then measure how far the zip tie was pushed up. That is the travel you used.
  4. If the bump stop is fully compressed in normal driving, you need more bump travel.
Under 35 mm with damper length already set?

With a "Do not adjust" sticker, or the lower mount at the groove limit, you may need a stiffer spring rate for that ride height. Contact us for a recommendation.

No sticker? Balance bump and droop at the lower mount

Then reset ride height with the spring seat.

ChangeBump travelDroopThen
Lower mount down (damper longer)LessMoreSpring seat down the same amount
Lower mount up (damper shorter)MoreLessSpring seat up the same amount

Example: you have 25 mm of droop and want 50 mm → lower mount down 25 mm, spring seat down 25 mm. Ride height stays the same. Re-check bump travel after.

Minimum thread engagement

It is marked by the machined groove on the damper body. Never wind the lower mount down far enough to expose the groove. Re-check bump travel and droop after every ride height change.

Damper adjustment

The adjuster on top of each damper has about 32 clicks of range. Clockwise is firmer, counterclockwise is softer, and it stops at both ends.

  • Count from full firm. Turn clockwise gently until it stops: that is 0. Count clicks counterclockwise from there (1, 2, 3… to about 32, full soft).
  • Start from the setup sheet. Your recommended setting for each corner is on the setup sheet. Return to it whenever you lose track.
  • Change one end at a time, 2 clicks at a time, and drive the same road or track to compare.
  • Keep left and right the same unless you are corner-tuning on purpose.
  • Never force the adjuster past its stop. It is a needle valve and can be damaged.

Bilanx ONE, P2 and P3 adjusters

Bilanx dampers come in three levels. Each step up adds an adjuster, so you can tune more of the damper's behavior separately.

  1. Start from the settings on your setup sheet.
  2. Count every adjuster from full firm, the same as the single adjustable. Turn clockwise gently until it stops: that is 0. Count clicks counterclockwise from there.
  3. Never force a knob past its stop at either end.
  4. Set rebound first, then compression.
  5. Change one adjuster, at one end of the car, 1–2 clicks at a time, and drive the same road or track to compare.
  6. Keep left and right the same unless you are corner-tuning on purpose.
  7. Write down every change.
P3 tuning tips

If the car rolls, dives or squats too much, add low-speed compression. If it feels harsh or skips over bumps and curbs, soften high-speed compression.

Note

Route reservoir hoses so they cannot touch the tire, brakes or exhaust through full travel and full steering lock. Secure them with zip ties.

Remote reservoir quick disconnect

Warning

Never drive the car or cycle a damper with its reservoir disconnected. This damages the damper internals.

Warning

The reservoir is charged with high-pressure nitrogen. Release all pressure before disconnecting.

Disconnecting
  1. Unload the damper. Put the car on jack stands and disconnect the anti-roll bar so the wheels hang at full droop, or remove the damper from the car.
  2. Release all nitrogen pressure through the Schrader valve on the reservoir. Make sure no pressure is left.
  3. Disconnect the reservoir at the quick disconnect. Keep both sides of the fitting clean.
Reconnecting
  1. Check both sides of the fitting are clean, then connect the reservoir fully.
  2. With the damper at full droop, charge the reservoir with nitrogen only (never air) to 180 psi using a gauge.
  3. The damper is ready to use.

05

Tuning guide

Fix handling one change at a time, starting with the first item in each row. Understeer means the front slides wide; oversteer means the rear steps out.

If the car…Try (in order)
Understeers on corner entrySofter front damping · firmer rear damping · raise rear height · firmer rear bar
Understeers mid-corner / exitFirmer rear damping · firmer rear bar · stiffer rear spring
OversteersSofter rear damping · lower rear height · softer rear bar · stiffer front spring
Lacks traction under powerSofter damping on the drive wheels · softer bar on the drive axle
Unsettled under brakingSofter front damping · firmer rear damping
Hits bump stops or bottoms outMore ride height · firmer damping · stiffer spring (last resort)
Feels harsh over bumpsSoften damping 2 clicks at a time on the harsh end
